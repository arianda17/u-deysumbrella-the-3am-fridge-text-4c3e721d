# The 3AM Fridge Text

A single text message from a smart fridge to its owner at 3am — filed as a formal complaint after twelve years of silent witness.

**Entry:**
> filing a formal complaint: 12 years of 3am stares and not once have you said good morning

## How it works

main.ts prints the fridge's 3am text to stdout and also writes it to /output/entry.txt. The entry is 89 characters, under the 140-character cap.
